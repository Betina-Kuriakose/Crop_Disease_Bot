Why CropAdvisor?

Reduce guesswork in farming decisions with AI-assisted guidance, while keeping the farmer fully in control of the final choice.

📊 Data-driven suggestions

Answers are based on an agricultural Q&A dataset with thousands of real queries, covering crops, soil, and common farm challenges.

🌐 Local language support

Farmers can ask questions in English or Indian languages. The system translates while keeping the meaning farmer-friendly.

🛡️ Safer health guidance

The chatbot avoids suggesting antibiotics or drug dosages. Instead, it focuses on hygiene, prevention, and when to consult a veterinarian or expert.

🚜 Built for small farmers

Designed to run on basic devices with simple UI, making digital advisory more accessible to small and marginal Indian farmers.
